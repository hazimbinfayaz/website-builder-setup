A family house, more than fifty years old. Reimagined in a classroom.

For over five decades our family has worked with the weavers and embroiderers of Kashmir, from a shop in Srinagar to patrons across India and beyond.

In 2023, in an entrepreneurship class at Ashoka University, the next generation asked how the whole world of Kashmiri arts and crafts could be reinvented. KwongPosh is the answer: the same hands, a new way to reach you.
