ii.Materials | مواد

Felt before
it is seen.

Beneath the lattice of a khatamband ceiling, three materials have shaped the Valley's hands for six centuries.

Pashmina

Weightless, then warm.

Combed by hand each spring from Changthangi goats on the high plateau, then spun on the yinder wheel — the craft that once gave the women of Kashmir their economic independence.

Walnut

Dense, cool, alive with grain.

Only fallen or aged trees are used. The heartwood is read like a manuscript before a single cut — the carver follows the grain, never fights it.

Saffron & Pigment

Kong posh — the saffron flower.

The bloom our name is taken from. Crocus from the karewas of Pampore, lapis and gold leaf — ground by hand into the palette of naqashi painters.
