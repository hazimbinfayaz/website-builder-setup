II  — The Materials

Felt before
it is seen.

Beneath the lattice of a khatamband ceiling, three materials have shaped the Valley's hands for six centuries.

Pashmina

Weightless, then warm.

Combed by hand each spring from Changthangi goats at 4,500 metres. Fibres of twelve microns, spun on the yinder wheel by women of the old city.

Walnut

Dense, cool, alive with grain.

Only fallen or aged trees are used. The heartwood is read like a manuscript before a single cut — the carver follows the grain, never fights it.

Saffron & Pigment

The colour of an October dawn.

Crocus stigmas from the karewas of Pampore, lapis, verdigris and gold leaf — ground by hand into the palette of naqashi painters.
