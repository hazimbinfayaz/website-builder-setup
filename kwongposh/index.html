کونگ پوش

The craft of Kashmir, traced to the hand.

One-of-one Kashmiri crafts, each geo-tagged to its artisan and paid for fairly. Opening first to a founding circle.

Join the circle Trace a piece

Kashmir's crafts are disappearing behind machines, middlemen and copies. We are building the opposite: a house where every piece exists once, carries the name of the hand that made it, and tells you exactly who was paid.

Four crafts to begin.

The founding collection draws on the Valley's oldest disciplines, chosen with the families who still practise them.

کانی

Kani Pashmina

Woven on a hand loom with wooden tojis, one weft colour at a time, following a coded pattern called the talim. A single shawl can keep a weaver at the loom for more than a year.

Kanihama, 34.07° N 74.62° E
سوزنی

Sozni embroidery

Needlework so fine it is often mistaken for weaving, worked by hand over months.

Srinagar, 34.08° N 74.80° E
اخروٹ

Walnut carving

Deep, undercut relief in seasoned Kashmiri walnut. The carver reads the grain first.

Anantnag, 33.73° N 75.15° E

Papier-mâché

Layered paper, burnished smooth with river stone, then painted in naqashi with hand-ground colour.

Zadibal, Srinagar, 34.11° N 74.81° E

    What comes with every piece.

    The artisan's insignia

    Every piece is signed by the hand that made it. You will know their name, their craft and their village.

    A geo-tagged SKU

    A record of every place and person in its making, from fibre or timber to the finished piece.

    An open price

    A pricing breakdown that shows what the artisan, the materials and the house each receive.

    A buyback option

    We will buy it back. A true heirloom should never lose its worth, or its way home.
